About Vaigai College Est. 2012

Vaigai College of Engineering (VCE) was established in 2012 under the aegis of the Vaigai Educational Trust (VET), Madurai, with the steadfast vision of delivering quality technical education and nurturing socially conscious global citizens. With a deep commitment to making cutting-edge engineering accessible to students from diverse socio-economic backgrounds, the institution champions value-based learning.

Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and permanently affiliated with Anna University, Chennai, VCE is set across a serene 15-acre lush green campus in Madurai. The campus boasts state-of-the-art academic complexes, lecture theatres, Dr. Abdul Kalam Auditorium, central library, advanced high-performance computing centers, dedicated engineering laboratories, sports grounds, residential hostels, and an active Training & Placement Cell.

About Vaigai College Est. 2012

Vaigai College of Engineering (VCE) was established in 2012 under the aegis of the Vaigai Educational Trust (VET), Madurai, with the steadfast vision of delivering quality technical education and nurturing socially conscious global citizens. With a deep commitment to making cutting-edge engineering accessible to students from diverse socio-economic backgrounds, the institution champions value-based learning.

Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and permanently affiliated with Anna University, Chennai, VCE is set across a serene 15-acre lush green campus in Madurai. The campus boasts state-of-the-art academic complexes, lecture theatres, Dr. Abdul Kalam Auditorium, central library, advanced high-performance computing centers, dedicated engineering laboratories, sports grounds, residential hostels, and an active Training & Placement Cell.
