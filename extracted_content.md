About Vaigai College

Vaigai College of Engineering (VCE) was established in 2012 under the Vaigai Educational Trust (VET), Madurai, with the vision of providing quality technical education and nurturing socially responsible global citizens. With a commitment to making quality technical education accessible to students from diverse backgrounds, the institution promotes inclusive and value-based education. Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, and affiliated with Anna University, Chennai, VCE is spread across a serene 15-acre campus that provides a conducive environment for learning. The campus is equipped with well-designed academic and administrative facilities, including lecture halls, tutorial and drawing halls, an auditorium, library, computer centre, laboratories and spacious playgrounds. It also houses hostel facilities, a canteen and a training and placement cell to support students’ academic, professional and entrepreneurial development. Vaigai College strives to foster academic excellence, creativity, leadership, professional competence and social responsibility, inspiring young minds to become skilled, confident and responsible contributors to society.

Workshop on Embedded Systems and IoT – 27 July – 3 August 2026

The Departments of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) and Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) jointly organized a One-Week Workshop on Embedded Systems and IoT from 27 July to 3 August 2026 at ML-3 for III and IV year ECE and EEE students. The workshop was conducted in association with Caliber Virtual Technologies as the training partner. Expert trainers provided hands-on training in microcontroller programming, sensor interfacing, IoT connectivity, and real-time application development. A total of 38 students, comprising 32 ECE and 6 EEE students, actively participated. The workshop enhanced the students’ practical, technical, and problem-solving skills and enabled them to develop basic microcontroller- and sensor-based IoT applications. The workshop was co-ordinated by Dr. R. Sivaranjani, Principal & Prof/ECE, Mr. A. Shakil Ahmed, AP/ECE & Ms. V.Saranya, AP/EEE.

State-Level Online Quiz Competition on HIV/STI Awareness – 12 August 2026



![Image](word/media/image22.jpeg)

The Red Ribbon Club (RRC) organized the TNSACS Quiz Competition – 2026, a State-Level Online Quiz Competition, on 12 August 2026 under the Intensified IEC Campaign 3.0. More than 140 students participated in the competition, which focused on HIV/STI prevention, the HIV/AIDS (Prevention and Control) Act, 2017, HIV testing and treatment, adolescent health, public health, and general knowledge. The quiz comprised 50 multiple-choice questions to be answered within 20 minutes. Attractive prizes were offered to the winners, and the programme effectively strengthened students’ awareness of HIV/STI prevention, testing, treatment, and responsible health practices. The programme was coordinated by Mr. C. Balaguru, AP/Mech, RRC Coordinator.
